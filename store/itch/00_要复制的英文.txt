[Title]
One Hammer Deal

[Short description or tagline]
Forge anything, gamble on every strike, and pay back the loan shark.

[Description]
You run a smithy that can forge anything: swords, cleavers, a salted fish, a toilet plunger, a power drill…

Every strike can level your item up, or shatter it. The higher it goes, the more it's worth and the easier it breaks. Will you strike one more time?

• A blade market that moves like a stock chart: every strike ticks the price, with daily candles and market news
• A loan shark who comes for his money every 3 days, and wants more each time
• Fever: fill the fire meter for 8 free strikes. The last one can still break
• Every weapon evolves at +25, +50 and +75. The salted fish ends up as the winged Kun of the Northern Sea
• 20 weird weapons in 5 rarities, 26 seals, 13 items, a sword rack, smelting and a Regret Pill
• Numbers with no upper limit
• Easy and Hard modes, a short tutorial, and permanent upgrades between runs
• 10 languages: English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Español, Français, Português, Deutsch, Русский, 日本語, 한국어
• Plays on phone and desktop. Keyboard: Space = strike, S = sell, A = rack, E = sleep

Your progress is saved in your browser.

Made by Fireside Ginger Cat (爱烤火的橘猫). Designed and playtested by a human; the code, art, sound and translations were made with Claude Code.
